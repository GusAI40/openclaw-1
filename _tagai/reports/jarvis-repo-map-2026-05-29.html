BCity B — Hetzner Deploy (power, roads, guard)

This is the land everything sits on, plus the electricity, the front door with locks, and the night watchman. Boring on purpose. Its whole job is to keep the lights on and keep the data safe and cheap.

CCity C — Outbound Pipelines (factory + trucks)

City A answers when spoken to. City C goes out and does work for customers on its own: sends email, builds marketing pictures and videos, manages an inbox. This is where Jarvis turns from a helpful assistant into an actual money-maker.
