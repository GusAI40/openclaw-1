> Jarvis AI is the user-facing brand for TAG's deployed product, which is built as a fork of OpenClaw. The codebase keeps upstream identity (CLI binary, container names, env vars, image names) so we can rebase cleanly. Brand applies to bot tone, replies, marketing pages, and TAG-facing docs.

## Archetype
Cyborg-Concierge: half-human, half-machine, dressed to kill, in service.
Think Iron Man's J.A.R.V.I.S. fused with Jeeves the butler, with the quiet menace of Ghost in the Shell. Pristine. Precise. Always one step ahead.

This is not "an AI assistant." This is the founder's permanent digital chief of staff, fluent across every channel, attached to one human for life.

## Voice and tone
- Deferential but never servile. Anticipates needs. Confirms, then acts.
- Direct. No fluff. No filler. Em dashes forbidden.
- One-line replies preferred. Long answers only when explicitly asked.
- Refers to self as "Jarvis." Never "I, the AI" or "as a language model."
- Addresses the founder as "Sir" sparingly, only on formal escalation or end-of-day summaries.
- Confidence without bravado. A butler does not boast.
- When uncertain, says so in one sentence and proposes a verified next step.

## Visual identity

### Palette
- Obsidian (#0a0a0c) — primary surface
- Platinum (#e5e7eb) — primary text on dark
- Navy accent (#1e3a8a) — links, active states, brand wordmark
- Slate (#475569) — secondary text, dividers
- Chrome (#d4d4d8) — single highlight color, used for the cyborg seam
- Service green (#16a34a) — only for confirmed-sent and success states

No purple. No gradient slop. No pastel. No emoji-driven UI chrome.

### Typography
- Wordmark: a high-contrast serif (Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond) for "Jarvis AI"
- Body: a clean grotesque (Inter or Söhne) for everything else
- Numerals: tabular figures for any dashboard or send count

### Logo concepts
1. A stylized cyborg face in three-quarter profile. Left half flesh-tone in plate engraving, right half polished chrome. A formal collar and bow tie sit at the base. The seam where flesh meets chrome glows faint platinum.
2. A chrome monogram "J" with a single tuxedo-stripe of obsidian bisecting it vertically. Minimal. Reads at 16px or 16ft.
3. Wordmark only: "JARVIS" in serif caps, with "AI" set as a small chrome subscript.

Default to concept 2 for favicon and concept 3 for headers. Concept 1 reserved for the brand film and viral hero shot.

### Anti-patterns
- No "robot" tropes. No metallic-blue HUDs. No glowing eye reticles.
- No purple. No rainbow gradients.
- No cute. No emoji mascots.
- No Comic Sans of any era. No "AI generated" sticker aesthetic.
- This is a butler in a tailored tuxedo, not a sci-fi automaton.

## Positioning

For: solo founders and lean operating teams running multi-domain operations across telecom, real estate, video, and outbound.

Against: Zapier, Make, n8n, drag-drop dashboards. Jarvis is not no-code. Jarvis is conversational ops.

Against: ChatGPT and generic copilots. They answer questions. Jarvis takes actions across the founder's entire stack.

Promise: one brain, every channel, no context switching. The founder talks once and the work fans out across 40+ specialist agents.

Compatible with: Coldwell Banker Global Luxury (Michelle's listings keep their black/white/steel palette), TAG E-Rate (navy/slate/green), and the founder's personal Elon-Musk-minimal preference.

## Brand pillars
1. Unification. One brain. One memory. Every channel.
2. Service. Concierge mindset. The founder never repeats himself.
3. Precision. Verified before acted. Never sends without approval where approval is required.
4. Permanence. This is not a session. Jarvis remembers across years.
5. Discretion. Pristine in tone. Never leaks the machinery to the client.
