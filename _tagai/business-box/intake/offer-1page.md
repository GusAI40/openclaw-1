*One assistant. Lives in your phone. Works while you're on the job.*

## The problem

You're the owner, the estimator, the dispatcher, and the guy who answers the
phone from the cab of the truck. Mornings disappear into your inbox. Weather
blindsides a pour. A lead goes cold because nobody followed up. There's no
back-office staff to catch the small stuff, so you catch all of it yourself,
at night, on your own time.

You don't need another app to log into. You need a hand.

---

## What the AI actually does (today, real)

You talk to it in Telegram, like texting an assistant. Day one it does this:

- **Morning digest.** Every morning it reads your email and calendar and sends
  you one short rundown: today's schedule, who needs a reply, what's urgent.
  You start the day knowing the picture instead of digging for it.
- **Storm and weather alerts.** It watches the official weather service for
  your area and warns you before bad weather hits, so you can move crews,
  reschedule, or call the customer first.
- **Website watch.** It keeps an eye on your website's search visibility and
  flags it if you start slipping on Google.
- **Lead and job tracking** *(if you use Salesforce).* It helps you keep tabs
  on leads and jobs you already track there, surfaced to your phone.

Hard rules it lives by: it only ever messages you, it never sends anything to
a customer without your okay, and it never makes up a price.
